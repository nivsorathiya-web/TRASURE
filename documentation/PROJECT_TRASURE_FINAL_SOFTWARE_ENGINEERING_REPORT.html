Executive Summary & Certificate

This final engineering deliverable documents the architectural design, formal requirements, implementation, and empirical verification of Project TRASURE.

Project Abstract

Rapid industrialization across Indian manufacturing clusters generates over 150 million metric tonnes of secondary by-products annually—predominantly fly ash, blast furnace slag, phosphogypsum, red mud, and spent acids. Despite significant economic and chemical reuse potential, these materials are predominantly discarded in unlined slurry ponds and landfills due to three structural friction points: (1) high search and discovery friction across fragmented corporate plants, (2) unpredictable long-haul road freight economics, and (3) cumbersome State Pollution Control Board (SPCB) regulatory tracking. TRASURE resolves these challenges through a unified, production-deployed platform integrating real-time geographic turn-by-turn routing via OSRM, landed-cost transparency engines, decremental reverse auction bidding with automated anti-sniping protection, and legally binding SHA-256 encrypted Form 10 hazardous waste manifests.

03. PROBLEM STATEMENT & SCOPE
01 INDUSTRIAL PROBLEM STATEMENT & SCOPE

Industrial Symbiosis & Problem Formulation

Heavy manufacturing industries in India face acute environmental and economic friction in secondary by-product valorization.

Structural Industry Friction

  • Severe Information Asymmetry: A thermal power plant in Madhya Pradesh producing 50,000 MT of Fly Ash has no real-time discovery channel to a cement plant in Gujarat needing pozzolanic binder.
  • Delivered Freight Opacity: By-products carry low ex-plant unit costs, meaning freight represents up to 70% of total delivered landed cost. Without GIS road route computation, buyers cannot evaluate commercial viability.
  • Asymmetric Price Discovery: Static negotiated procurement leads to monopolistic middlemen skimming margins and artificially depressing generator recovery rates.
  • Regulatory Tracking Overhead: Compliance with SPCB Form 10 hazardous manifest tracking is predominantly manual, creating paper-trail liabilities.

The TRASURE Solution

  • Algorithmic Landed Cost Engine: Computes exact delivered gate pricing in real time combining ex-plant price, OSRM road distance, dynamic ton-kilometer freight rates, and clearing fees.
  • Real-Time Decremental Reverse Auction: Allows buyers to launch procurement tenders where vetted sellers submit downward counter-bids with automated 60-second anti-sniping extensions.
  • Interactive Leaflet GIS Logistics Viewport: Renders high-precision highway corridors, plant pin coordinates, and toll allowances across India.
  • Cryptographic SPCB Manifest Vault: Auto-generates legally valid Form 10 manifests with SHA-256 integrity hashes.

15. ALGORITHMIC ENGINEERING
08 ALGORITHMIC LOGIC & MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION

Algorithmic Engineering & Mathematical Engines

Formal formulations of TRASURE's spatial routing fallback, landed cost optimization, and anti-sniping scheduling algorithms.

1. Haversine Spatial Fallback Engine

Calculates great-circle distance between generator $(\phi_1, \lambda_1)$ and buyer $(\phi_2, \lambda_2)$ when OSRM API is unreachable:

Δφ = (φ2 - φ1) × π / 180
Δλ = (λ2 - λ1) × π / 180
a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ1)cos(φ2)sin²(Δλ/2)
c = 2 × atan2(√a, √(1-a))
RoadDistance_km = R × c × 1.25 (Road Curvature Factor)

2. Delivered Landed Cost Optimization

Computes gate-delivered unit economics for procurement decision ranking:

FreightRate = ₹2.01 / km / MT
FreightCost = RoadDistance_km × FreightRate
BaseExPlant = Listing.exPlantPricePerMT
PlatformClearing = (BaseExPlant + FreightCost) × 0.02
TotalLandedCost = BaseExPlant + FreightCost + PlatformClearing

3. Anti-Sniping Dynamic Timer Extension Algorithm

function processIncomingBid(auctionLot, incomingBid) {
  if (incomingBid.amount >= auctionLot.currentLeadingBid) return { success: false, reason: "Must undercut leading bid" };
  
  auctionLot.currentLeadingBid = incomingBid.amount;
  auctionLot.leadingBidderId = incomingBid.bidderId;
  auctionLot.bidsHistory.push(incomingBid);
  
  // Anti-Sniping Evaluation
  if (auctionLot.remainingSeconds <= 60) {
    auctionLot.remainingSeconds += 60; // Grant 60-second overtime window
    auctionLot.antiSnipingTriggered = true;
    broadcastAuctionAlert("ANTI_SNIPING_TRIGGERED", { lotId: auctionLot.id, newRemaining: auctionLot.remainingSeconds });
  }
  return { success: true, updatedLot: auctionLot };
}
